# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, generated by `build.py` from `data/menu.json` (user's choice, 2026-11-06). No framework, no server. Must also run as a claude.ai artifact (no iframes, fonts only from Google Fonts, images as local files).

## Users

People in Plovdiv and visitors choosing where to eat, meet, or celebrate. Typical scenes: on a phone deciding tonight's dinner (needs menu, prices, hours, a call button); a couple or family planning a wedding, birthday, or other celebration (needs to see the hall dressed for an event and to send an inquiry); someone booking a business lunch.

## Product Purpose

Website of Ресторант „Хитър Петър“, an established restaurant at ул. „Васил Априлов“ 140, Пловдив. It must make people want to come, show the full menu with prices, and turn interest into a phone reservation or an event inquiry.

## Positioning

A restaurant hidden "behind a wall of greenery", a few steps from a busy street, with its own summer garden and parking, a hall of 170 seats plus 120 outside, over 100 wines, and a long record of weddings and celebrations. The owners' own phrase: „пространството на неограничените желания“; guests choose the music on Fridays and Saturdays.

## Operating Context

Reservations happen by phone (+359 32 968 778). The daily lunch menu and offers are posted on Facebook. Open every day 10:00–01:00.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No reservation backend. The inquiry form prepares an e-mail to hitarpetar1@abv.bg and must say it is not a confirmed booking.
- Menu: 180 items transcribed from hitarpetar.net with portions and prices in EUR and BGN; seven items have no price on the source.
- All visible copy in Bulgarian.

## Brand Commitments

- Name „Хитър Петър“ and the existing logo (the folk trickster Hitar Petar riding a donkey, drinking from a cup), `assets/img/logo.png`.
- The owners' wording about the garden and „неограничените желания“.
- The palette in the original brief (forest green, ivory, burgundy, antique gold) is a suggestion; the user released it on 2026-11-06.

## Evidence on Hand

- 23 of the restaurant's own photographs (`assets/img/`): hall by day and evening, summer garden, terrace, façade, table details, 11 wedding set-ups. Venue photos are 900×599, wedding photos 1500×1000.
- No food photography exists. Do not use stock food photos as if they were this kitchen.
- Real reviews exist on the old site but were not cleared for reuse; do not invent testimonials, awards, or ratings.

## Product Principles

1. The place sells itself: real photographs of the hall and garden carry the page.
2. Calling and finding the restaurant is never more than one tap away.
3. Celebrations are a second business of equal weight to dinner.
4. Every fact (price, hour, capacity) is the restaurant's own.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The client's own machine runs with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Motion must remain visible and expressive in that mode through non-vestibular means (opacity, clip, blur, colour) while dropping parallax and large movement.
